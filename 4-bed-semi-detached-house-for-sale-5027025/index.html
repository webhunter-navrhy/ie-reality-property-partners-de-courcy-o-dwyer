About the property

Description property

Property Partners de Courcy O Dwyer are delighted to bring to the market this substantial family home which is nestled in this mature estate overlooking a large green area.

The extended accommodation comprises of entrance porch, entrance hall, living room, sitting room, dining room, kitchen / breakfast room, guest w.c., four bedrooms and garage. The property is ideally positioned adjacent to the Crescent Shopping Centre and within walking distance of UHL and within close proximity to the city centre and Raheen Industrial Estate.

Outside the property is further complimented with a very large enclosed mature rear garden and a front garden with off street parking and facing on to a large green area.

A viewing of this property is highly recommended.
